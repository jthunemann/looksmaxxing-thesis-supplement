Text Mining Looksmaxxing Forum Discourse

Interactive figures and aggregated results for a master thesis on publicly accessible English-language looksmaxxing forum threads.

Data availability and ethics note: This page does not contain raw forum text, usernames, thread URLs, thread IDs, direct quotes, images, or profile information. Only aggregated, deidentified results and visualisations are shared.

Project overview

This thesis examines dominant topics, sentiment patterns, and exploratory emotion signals in publicly accessible looksmaxxing forum discourse. The unit of analysis is the whole thread.

Analysis pipeline

  1. Collection of public forum threads
  2. Text cleaning and deidentification
  3. BERTopic topic modelling
  4. Manual topic validation and labelling
  5. RoBERTa-based sentiment analysis
  6. Exploratory multi-label emotion classification
  7. Aggregated visualisation of results
